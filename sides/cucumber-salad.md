# Cucumber salad

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- A pinch of salt
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Slice the cucumber thinly.
2. Mix the rice vinegar, sesame oil, and salt.
3. Toss the cucumber with the dressing.
4. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve.
