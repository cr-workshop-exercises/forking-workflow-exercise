# Salmon soup recipe

## Ingredients

- **500 g** salmon fillet, skin removed, cut into cubes
- **800 g** potatoes, peeled and diced
- **1** carrot, sliced
- **1** onion, finely chopped
- **1** liter fish stock or water
- **2** bay leaves
- **5** whole allspice berries (optional)
 -**200 ml** heavy cream
- **2** tbsp butter
- Fresh dill, chopped
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

- Melt the butter in a large pot and gently sauté the onion for 3–4 minutes.
- Add the fish stock (or water), potatoes, carrot, bay leaves, and allspice. Bring to a boil.
- Reduce heat and simmer for 10–15 minutes, until the potatoes are almost tender.
- Add the salmon cubes and cook gently for 4–5 minutes, until the fish is just cooked through.
- Stir in the cream and heat without boiling.
- Season with salt and black pepper.
- Remove the bay leaves and stir in plenty of fresh dill.
