# PBJ Sandwich Recipe


## Ingredients

- **2** slices of bread
- Peanut butter
- Jam
- **1/2** banana


## Instructions

1. Slice a banana into several pieces.
2. Spread peanut butter on one slice of bread.
2. Spread jam on the other slice and put the banana slices on it.
3. Put the slices together and gently press them.
