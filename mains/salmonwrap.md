# Salmon wrap

## Ingredients
- Salmon
- Tortilla
- Mango
- Cucumber
- Sourcream
- Mango salsa
- Red onion

## Instructions
- Cook the salmon in the oven or the pan.
- Cut the cucumber, mango, and onion into pieces.
- Put everything into the tortilla.
- Enjoy.
