# Spicy Spaghetti Recipe

## Ingredients
- 200g passata
- tomato puree
- 200g spaghetti
- olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 red chillies
- chilli flakes, salt and pepper
- 10 cherry tomatoes

## Instructions
- heat the passata and tomato puree in a saucepan
- add the garlic, chillies and tomatoes
- boil the pasta
- mix together, season and drizzle with olive oil
