# Chicken salad

This salad is meant as a main dish. Use the amounts you prefer.

## Ingredients
- Chicken, chopped (for example leftovers from a whole roasted chicken)
- Baked potato wedges
- Apple(s)
- Cashews
- Bell peppers, chopped
- Cucumber, sliced
- Baby spinach
- Lemon juice
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
- Chop the apple and mix with lemon juice (to avoid that the apple gets brown).
- Mix together chicken, potatoes, bell peppers, cucumber, apples, cashews, and baby spinach in a bowl.
- Add olive oil, salt, and pepper.
