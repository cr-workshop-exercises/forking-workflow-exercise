# Cardamom Baked Apples

## Ingredients

- 4 firm apples
- 40 g rolled oats
- 30 g chopped almonds
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 25 g butter, softened
- 100 ml water

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 190 C.
2. Remove the apple cores while leaving the bottoms intact.
3. Mix the oats, almonds, sugar, cardamom, cinnamon, and butter.
4. Fill each apple with the oat mixture and place them in a baking dish.
5. Pour the water into the dish and bake for 30 to 35 minutes.
6. Serve warm when the apples are tender but still hold their shape.
