# Chocolate mug cake

## Ingredients

- **2 tablespoons** flour
- **2 tablespoons** cocoa powder
- **2 tablespoons** sugar
- **1/8 teaspoon** salt
- **1/4 teaspoon** baking powder
- **1 tablespoon** canola oil
- **3 tablespoons** oat drink or milk
- **1/2 teaspoon** vanilla extract
- **1 tablespoon** chocolate chips

## Instructions

- Mix the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and baking powder in a mug.
- Add in the oil, oat drink, and vanilla, and mix well.
- Sprinkle the chocolate chips on top.
- Cook in the microwave for 1 minute and enjoy.
