# Cinnamon Buns

## Ingredients

- 500 g flour
- 250 ml milk
- 75 g butter
- 75 g sugar
- 7 g dry yeast
- 1 egg
- 50 g brown sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Warm the milk and mix with the yeast.
2. Add flour, sugar, egg, and melted butter.
3. Knead the dough and let it rise for 1 hour.
4. Roll out the dough into a rectangle.
5. Spread with butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon.
6. Roll up and cut into individual buns.
7. Let rise for another 30 minutes.
8. Bake at 190°C for 15–20 minutes.
