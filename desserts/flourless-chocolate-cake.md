# Flourless Chocolate Fudge Mousse Cake

## Ingredients

- 240 g unsalted butter
- 360 g dark chocolate (min. 70% cocoa), finely chopped
- 290 g light brown sugar
- 4 tbsp water
- 5 large eggs, separated
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 170 °C.
2. Put the butter and chocolate in a large heatproof bowl.
3. Bring the sugar and water to a boil in a saucepan over medium heat. Pour the hot syrup over the butter and chocolate and stir until everything has melted into a smooth, runny sauce.
4. Let the mixture cool a little, then stir in the egg yolks one at a time.
5. Whisk the egg whites with the salt until they just hold stiff peaks.
6. Fold the whites into the chocolate mixture in three additions with a metal spoon, keeping as much air in as possible.
7. Pour about 2/3 of the batter (roughly 800 g) into a baking tin. Bake for 30–40 minutes, until a skewer in the centre comes out almost clean.
8. Let the base cool slightly, then pour the remaining batter on top. Bake for another 15–20 minutes, until a skewer brings up moist crumbs but no liquid batter.
9. Cool completely before releasing from the tin.
